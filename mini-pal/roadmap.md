# Mini Pal: roadmap (PROPOSAL v0.1)

Durations are rough guesses. Many steps overlap with [Pal](../pal/roadmap.md) and can be done once for both.

| # | Phase | Done when | Est. |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Breadboard proof** | ESP32-C3 + 2.0" screen: face at 20 fps, BLE link to a phone, iPhone notifications (ANCS), chirp, scroll wheel works | 2-3 weeks |
| 2 | **Firmware MVP** | Widgets (face, clock, weather, calendar, notification, night), rotation, BLE messages, chirp | 4-6 weeks |
| 2b | **NRX phone app MVP** (in parallel) | iPhone + Android: pairing, widget picker, weather (WeatherKit / API), calendar, notification filters, Android notification forwarding | 6-10 weeks |
| 3 | **Box prototypes** | CAD + prints: even bezel, stable foot, matte finish like the concept | 3-6 weeks |
| 4 | **Custom PCB** | Small board behind the screen, 5 sets tested | 3-5 weeks |
| 5 | **Certification + legal** | CE (radio, EMC, RoHS), weather licence, App Store + Google Play review, privacy policy | 1-3 months |
| 6 | **Small batch** | 20-50 units for testers, then launch | – |

**Shared with Pal:** face engine, screen choice, case finish, NRX app, test lab contact.
