# Mini Pal: business (draft)

## Pitch

> **Mini Pal: a tiny face for your desk.** It tells you the time and the weather, waves at your notifications, and is simply cute to have around.

## Who it is for

- Same people as Pal (desk setups, students, remote workers, gamers), but as an **impulse buy or gift**
- People who find Pal too expensive, or don't want a mic on their desk
- People who want a cute notification display for their phone on the desk

## Why make it

- **Cheaper and faster to build than Pal:** no voice, no body, no PC app needed.
- It **proves the shared parts early**: face engine, screen, case finish, the NRX phone app, CE testing of a radio device. Pal reuses all of it.
- It can launch **before Pal** and bring in money and feedback.
