# Idea & Story

## The idea
RK Industrie makes fully modular, customizable pegboard panels for desks and workspaces: 3D printed, with integrated RGB lighting and a wide range of clip-on components (keyboard tray, controller holders, headset hook, mouse dock, phone holder, bottle holder, pen cups, cable hooks, monitor shelf, plant/figurine shelves).

**Website configurator:** customers pre-design their own panel online (size, layout, colors, RGB style, components) and order exactly what they built.

## Core selling points
- **Solid, modular mounting**: parts attach securely and can be moved anytime (inductive/magnetic fixation is being explored, see prototypes)
- **Integrated RGB**: customizable lighting (Arduino / embedded controller)
- **Multiple slots**: keyboard, controllers, headset, mouse, accessories
- **Adapts to your space**: expandable and reconfigurable
- **Designed by makers, for makers**

Tagline: *Build. Organize. Play. Better.*
