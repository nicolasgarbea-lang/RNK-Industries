# Pal: business (draft)

## Pitch

> **Pal: a little friend on your monitor that takes care of you.**
> It reminds you to drink, to take breaks, and what's on your to-do list. Just talk to it.

## Who it is for

- People who sit at a PC all day: remote workers, students, gamers, developers
- People who forget to drink water and take breaks
- Fans of cute desk setups (the same audience as the NRX pegboard)

## Why it is different

- **Physical and cute**: a real character on your desk, not another notification on screen.
- **Zero setup**: plug it into the PC, and it turns on and off with the PC.
- **Privacy**: physical mic mute switch. The voice runs through the user's own app.
- **Fits the NRX world**: matches NRX desk setups (a pegboard mount for Pal could come later).
